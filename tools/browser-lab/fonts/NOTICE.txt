Self-hosted webfonts for the browser lab (COEP require-corp cannot load
typical CDN fonts). All three families are SIL Open Font License 1.1.

- Inter (latin variable): https://github.com/rsms/inter
- JetBrains Mono (latin variable): https://github.com/JetBrains/JetBrainsMono
- Symbols Nerd Font (Powerline/status glyphs only): https://github.com/ryanoasis/nerd-fonts
  Subset built with fontTools from NerdFontsSymbolsOnly Regular, covering
  U+E0A0–U+E0DF plus a few status icons (user, terminal, lock).
